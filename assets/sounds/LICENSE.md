# Sound licenses

All sounds here (and the copies in `templates/*/Content/Sounds/`) are synthesized by
`tools/MakeSounds.cpp` (`cmake --build <build> --target MakeSounds`, then `MakeSounds assets/sounds`).
They contain no recorded or third-party material and are dedicated to the public domain
([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)).
